FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

The project

Is MoneroUSD live?

The public testnet is live — join it with free test coins. Real funds come only after the testnet, every listed attack refused on real nodes, an outside audit, and a public soak with a bug bounty.

Is MoneroUSD part of the Monero Project?

No. It's a separate project with its own chain and cryptography. It shares the goal of private money, and its wallet trades directly with Monero (XMR) and Bitcoin (BTC).

Who controls the network?

Nobody in particular. There is one kind of node, which mines, verifies, proves and relays. Every key that moves value — fees, rewards, custody, bonds — is per-participant or held by a threshold of bonded validators, never one shared key.

Privacy & security

How are amounts, senders and receivers hidden?

Money lives in confidential notes. To spend, your wallet proves in zero knowledge that the spend is valid; a one-time nullifier enters the spent set, so a note can't be spent twice. The chain stores commitments, nullifiers and proofs — never amounts or addresses — and every block has the same shape, so activity can't be counted.

What does "post-quantum" mean here?

Key exchange uses ML-KEM-768, signatures ML-DSA-65, commitments Poseidon2, proofs WHIR, hashing BLAKE3 and SHA-3. There are no elliptic curves anywhere, so traffic recorded today can't be decrypted later. The one exception is verifying another chain's own signatures at the Bitcoin and Monero bridge, which is unavoidable and clearly separated.

Does my node see my balance?

No. Your scan key never leaves your device — not even to your own node. Notes are found by your wallet locally.

Can a payment be reversed?

No. Payments are final: no chargebacks, no recovery from a wrong address. The wallet warns you in plain language and shows every cost in dollars before you confirm.

USDm & SAT

What is USDm?

The dollar of MoneroUSD. Every USDm is minted against a Bitcoin or Monero deposit and the system is over-collateralized. It's what you pay with, stake and trade.

What is SAT?

The reward asset: 21,000,000 maximum, 19,000,000 issued only by block rewards halving about every four years, and a developer set-aside capped at 2,000,000 that is proven at genesis with the exact amount private. See SAT tokenomics.

What does a transaction cost?

A flat $0.001 network fee per transaction, the same for everyone so the fee reveals nothing. It's split between USDm stakers (40%), SAT for validators' bonds (25%), the surplus buffer (25%) and miners (10%, rising later). First-party apps may add an interface fee, always shown in dollars first.

Features

How does tap-to-pay work without a card network?

The merchant's wallet advertises an ML-DSA-signed charge over Bluetooth LE. The customer's wallet, within a few centimetres, completes an ML-KEM handshake, shows the signed amount in dollars, and pays on approval. The radio only carries ciphertext.

What is Letter?

Private messaging inside the wallet: letters sealed end to end with ML-KEM-768 and signed with ML-DSA-65, payments inside the chat, groups and profiles. No phone number, email or server; history restores from your seed.

What is Ion Swap?

The private DeFi layer. Orders are sealed, the bonded validators clear each batch on secret shares, and the chain publishes only one clearing price per pair per batch — never volumes, depth, positions or who traded. Settlement is proven, so validators can't steal or misprice. Read more.

What is Dark Solidity?

MoneroUSD's smart-contract language and zero-knowledge virtual machine. Your wallet runs a contract call and proves it ran correctly; every node verifies the proof without re-running the contract or seeing its inputs. Values stay commitments, everything is post-quantum, and a contract can't mint or hold funds. Read more.

What is the MoneroUSD Veil?

MoneroUSD's own post-quantum mix network: three-hop layered packets, constant cover traffic, private transaction origin and private reads. It's partly built and in testing. Read more.

What is Sovereign?

A browser for websites that live on the network: publish in three taps, own the site with your wallet key, and every byte is checked against the chain. Parts are built; .mud names and sharded hosting are in design. Read more.

Will phones run full nodes?

That's the design goal: node state stays the same size as the chain grows, and a new node joins at the tip by checking one recursive proof. It's being measured on real nodes.
