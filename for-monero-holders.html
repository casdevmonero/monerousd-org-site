For Monero holders

The privacy you expect. Built for what comes next.

You already know why private money matters. MoneroUSD carries that idea forward: post-quantum cryptography throughout, a private dollar you can mint with XMR, and privacy for the network and metadata, not just the ledger.

In testing · not yet live

Post-quantum throughout

Monero's cryptography today is elliptic-curve based. MoneroUSD uses ML-KEM, ML-DSA, Poseidon2 and WHIR — no elliptic curves — so there's nothing to record now and break later.

A private dollar, minted with XMR

Deposit Monero and get USDm, the over-collateralized dollar of MoneroUSD, for when you want to hold or spend dollars without leaving privacy behind.

Trade SAT directly for XMR

A direct SAT/XMR pool: buy SAT with Monero or sell and be paid in Monero. The chain publishes one clearing price per batch — no volume, depth or positions.

Metadata counts too

Every block has the same shape, so activity can't be counted. The MoneroUSD Veil mixes network traffic with constant cover, and your transaction never leaves from your own node.

Messages and payments together

Letter is sealed messaging in the wallet with payments inside the chat — no phone number, no email, no server.

Your scan key stays home

Your wallet finds its own notes locally. The scan key never leaves your device, not even to your own node.

A separate project, honestly described

MoneroUSD is not part of the Monero Project and doesn't change Monero. It's in development and testing; the public testnet is live, and real funds wait for an outside audit and a public soak.
