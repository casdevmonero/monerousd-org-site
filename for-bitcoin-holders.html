For Bitcoin holders

Keep your Bitcoin. Spend in private.

Bitcoin is a public ledger: every payment you make tells the world your balance and your history. MoneroUSD gives your BTC a private, post-quantum place to spend from.

In testing · not yet live

Private dollars against your BTC

Deposit Bitcoin and get USDm, the over-collateralized dollar of MoneroUSD. From there, every payment hides the amount, the sender and the receiver.

One private deposit address

"Add money with Bitcoin" gives you one address. With Auto-OPSEC on by default, the wallet mixes what arrives before it deposits, and warns you plainly if you turn that off.

Borrow without selling

Take USDm against your Bitcoin and repay any time, with every cost shown in dollars first. Your BTC exposure stays.

Trade SAT directly for BTC

SAT, the network's reward asset, has a direct SAT/BTC pool: buy with BTC, or sell and be paid in BTC. Like Bitcoin, its supply is capped at 21 million.

Pay in person

Tap-to-pay over Bluetooth LE: the merchant signs the charge, you approve the amount in dollars, and it settles on chain. No card network, no chargebacks.

Built for the quantum era

Every key exchange, signature and proof is post-quantum. Nothing on MoneroUSD relies on cryptography a quantum computer is expected to break.

Honest about where it stands

MoneroUSD is in development. The Bitcoin bridge mints USDm on real test nodes and refuses forged mints and burns, but nothing here holds real funds yet. The public testnet is live; real funds wait for an outside audit and a public soak. Bridge custody is designed around validators' SAT bonds: a validator that signs a theft loses its bond, and the bond repays the victims.
