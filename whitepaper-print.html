Private digital cash is a right. So I am building it.

Cash never asked who you were. It kept no diary of your life, it could not be switched off, and when you handed it over the matter was finished. That kind of money is being taken from us one convenience at a time. What replaces it watches every purchase, answers to whoever controls it, and melts a little in your hand each year. I refuse to accept that as the future. In 2008 Satoshi Nakamoto proved that money does not need a master, and called it electronic cash. MoneroUSD is my attempt to finish that sentence: cash rebuilt for the internet, private and yours the way a banknote in your pocket is yours, with a whole economy around it that nobody can close to you. This page is the reason I am building it.

02 Three rights

  • Private digital cash. What you earn and what you buy is nobody's business. Every payment is checked by every node and readable by none: not the amount, the sender or the receiver.
  • No censor. No account to open, no approval, no freeze switch, no payment pulled back. Nodes speak only over hidden addresses.
  • The right to a financial system. Saving, paying, borrowing, trading and selling, open to any phone on earth, with nobody deciding who qualifies.

03 Money nobody prints

Inflation is a decision. Take the decision away.

It is a choice, made in rooms you will never be invited into, by people it does not hurt. Wages and savings pay for it, a few percent at a time.

I want a world where your pay goes up and your prices do not follow it, where saving works again and someone who starts with nothing has a real shot at wealth.

So MoneroUSD has no printer. Every USDm is minted against a larger deposit of Bitcoin or Monero, checked by proof. Beside it sits SAT, named for Satoshi and capped for good at 21,000,000

Take away the power to print and the power to freeze. That is how inflation ends: by giving people the exit.

04 The design

I set these rules before the first line, and never traded one for another.

  1. Private by construction. Nothing to look at: no amounts, no names, no links, no counts.
  2. Post-quantum from the first byte. ML-KEM ML-DSA Poseidon2 WHIR.
  3. Proof, never trust. Sealed notes, one-time spend markers, zero-knowledge proofs.
  4. Small enough for a phone. Parallel blocks fold into one recursive proof, so the node never grows.
  5. Nobody to ask, and it never stops. No company or server to depend on.

05 Five convictions

  • Privacy and protection belong together.Private, final payments, with an escrow that no company judges.
  • Crypto does not need permission to be safe.Safety is built, never granted. Every rule that begins as protection ends as reach.

07 Who it is for

The worker whose raise vanished. The shop paying three percent to sell. The person a bank did not want. The saver. Anyone whose life is their own.

Regulated away, or real.

Crypto began as cash for the internet. Then most of it took a deal: hold your customers' keys, collect their passports, add a freeze button, and you may stay in business. This industry has to decide what it is. I choose real. I want a world where pay rises and prices do not, where nobody can be locked out of money, and where the most powerful technology we have ever made protects people instead of watching them. I believe that world can be built, so I am building it.
